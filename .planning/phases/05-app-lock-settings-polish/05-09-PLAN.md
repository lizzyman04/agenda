<objective>
Make D-07 visible on the lock screen: how many attempts remain, the shake on a wrong PIN, the
escalating cooldown with a live countdown that survives an app kill, and the storage-error
message. Thin path first: a wrong PIN shows "Restam N tentativas" and the dots shake.

Purpose: the ladder (05-02) and its persistence (05-03) protect the user only if the person
at the keypad understands why it stopped accepting input; a silent clear reads as a bug.

Output: LockMessage model and cooldown ticker in LockScreenCubit, LockStatusLine, PinDots
shake, disabled keypad, tests.
</objective>
