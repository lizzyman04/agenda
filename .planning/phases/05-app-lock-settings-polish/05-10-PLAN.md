<objective>
Add the native backstops behind the Flutter privacy overlay (D-04): Android FLAG_SECURE and an
iOS App Switcher cover, toggled over one method channel, and applied at every process start
when a PIN is configured. Thin path first: Dart setSecure(true) reaches the Android window flag.

Purpose: Flutter stops producing frames at hidden and iOS snapshots after the background
callback (RESEARCH Pattern 7), so the overlay alone cannot guarantee SC-2's "privacy overlay
appears in the iOS App Switcher"; FLAG_SECURE also blocks screenshots on Android.

Output: SecureWindowGateway + SecureWindowChannel, Kotlin and Swift handlers, the boot-time
apply in startAppLock, and contract tests. Toggling on PIN enable/disable is wired by 05-12's
PinLifecycle against this gateway.
</objective>
