<objective>
Deliver UX-03: a one-time, one-screen privacy statement shown before the main UI on first
launch, re-readable from Settings. Thin path first: the flag gates the root, "Começar" writes
the flag and enters the shell, and a second launch skips the screen.

Purpose: the statement is the first thing a user learns about AGENDA's privacy commitment
(SC-4); it must be shown exactly once and never leave the app half-entered.

Output: OnboardingGate, PrivacyStatementScreen (first-launch and read-only), PrivacyBullet,
PrivacyLanguageSwitch, READMEs, the app.dart home change, and tests.
</objective>
