<objective>
Wire the planners to the OS: a snapshot loader (uncapped reads), a localising content
builder, a reconcile runner that diffs desired against pending and applies the
difference, a debounced single-flight coordinator, the permission gate, the DI planner
composition and the lifecycle observer. After this plan a task or debt with a due date
produces a real reminder on a device.

Purpose: this is the end-to-end proof of the notification architecture (the phase's real
tracer). It implements `NotificationReconciler` (defined in 04-10) so the settings
cubit, evaluators and backup flow can depend on the interface.

Output: five infrastructure files, a DI module, startup wiring and four test files. UI,
tap-through and the immediate budget/goal evaluators are later plans.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Persisted schedule (OS) -> reconcile | The pending list is read back and compared; foreign or corrupt entries must not be destroyed. |
| Notification text -> lock screen | Localised strings leave the app. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| T-04-12-01 | Denial of service | A transient repository error cancelling every reminder | high | mitigate | Aborted reports change nothing; asserted by a runner test where the loader returns Err. |
| T-04-12-02 | Tampering | Evaluator-raised alerts cancelled by the diff | medium | mitigate | Only planner-owned ids enter the diff; non-owned entries are cancelled solely when their kind is disabled. |
| T-04-12-03 | Information disclosure | Task or debt titles in text | medium | mitigate | Text carries titles only (no amounts), visibility is private (04-10); titles clipped to 100 characters. |
| T-04-12-04 | Denial of service | Reconcile storm from watchLazy bursts | low | mitigate | 400 ms debounce and single-flight serialisation with at most one queued run. |
</threat_model>
