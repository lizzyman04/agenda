<objective>
Implement the two app-open evaluators the planner cannot cover: budget threshold alerts
(80% and 100%, on transaction and budget changes) and the on-open goal off-track alert,
plus the shared dispatcher that applies the toggle, permission gate and quiet-hours
deferral to an immediate alert.

Purpose: D-05 and D-06 define alerts that depend on in-app events, not on a future
timestamp, so a pre-scheduled one-shot cannot represent them. Fired state is persisted in
the alert-state store so restarts, undo/redo of transactions and imports cannot repeat
or storm alerts.

Output: one domain rule, one infrastructure dispatcher, two application evaluators, the
application README, startup wiring and four test files.
</objective>
